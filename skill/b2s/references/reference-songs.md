# Reference songs: Titanic and Two Hearts, Two Houses

The user called these two songs masterpieces. When a story is apt (a tragedy, a tragic love story, or a solemn historical event), write in the same way. These are models for shape, line length, hook, and tone. Never copy their lines into a new song.

## What they share

- **Shape:** 28 lines in seven sections: Verse, Chorus, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Verse, Chorus. The last chorus changes its lines to resolve the story and keeps the hook.
- **Plain, medium-length lines:** about 9 to 13 syllables (the two songs average about 11 to 12), in rhymed couplets, with simple words and one story beat per couplet.
- **A named hook** that sits in the first and last line of the chorus ("Two hearts, two houses," "Remember the Titanic").
- **Specific facts or scenes** in the verses (real dates, lifeboats, a ballroom, a Friar's plan), told in order.
- **Respectful and non-graphic.** The loss is implied, with no method details, and the ending offers a lesson or a peace.
- **Third-person storyteller voice,** not first person.
- **Sound:** a bittersweet acoustic ballad with fingerpicked or plain acoustic guitar, gentle piano, and cello; female and male voices trading lines for the love story, and one warm male lead with quiet harmonies for the historical event.

## Two Hearts, Two Houses (Romeo and Juliet)

Style that worked: `soft sweet bittersweet acoustic ballad, fingerpicked guitar, gentle piano and cello, steady mid-tempo around 95 BPM, female and male voices trading lines, warm harmonies on the chorus`

```
[Verse]
Verona, two proud houses, feuding year by year,
Romeo slips into a party, finds Juliet is near,
One look across the ballroom and the whole room fades away,
Two names that shouldn't love each other, but they do anyway

[Chorus]
Two hearts, two houses, a love too bright to hide,
Two names at war, but they stood side by side,
Under the moonlight, they promised to be true,
Nothing could keep them apart, or so they knew

[Verse]
The Friar wed them secretly, hoping peace would come,
But a street fight broke, and Romeo's friend was gone,
Romeo's anger struck down Juliet's cousin too,
Banished from Verona, with nothing left to do

[Chorus]
Two hearts, two houses, a love too bright to hide,
Two names at war, but they stood side by side,
Under the moonlight, they promised to be true,
Nothing could keep them apart, or so they knew

[Bridge]
Her father chose a husband she did not want to wed,
The Friar's plan: a sleep so deep they'd think she was dead,
But the message to Romeo never made it through,
He heard she was gone, and he rushed back too

[Verse]
He found her still, and his heart gave way,
She woke too late, with so much to say,
Two young lives ended in one sad night,
Under a pale and silent light

[Chorus]
Two hearts, two houses, a love too bright to hide,
Now the feud is over, and the families stand side by side,
Grief made them end what pride had kept alive,
Two names remembered, and a love that will survive
```

## Titanic (the real 1912 sinking)

Style that worked: `solemn soft folk ballad, acoustic guitar, gentle piano and cello, steady mid-tempo around 95 BPM, warm male lead vocal, quiet harmonies on the chorus`

```
[Verse]
April, nineteen twelve, a ship so grand,
The biggest ever built, the pride of the land,
Southampton to New York on her very first trip,
They called her unsinkable, a mighty ship

[Chorus]
Cold waters, cold waters, calling in the night,
A ship of dreams beneath the starry light,
Lifeboats lowered, and the band played on,
Remember the Titanic, though the ship is gone

[Verse]
Four days out, on a calm and freezing night,
A lookout spotted an iceberg, right ahead in sight,
The great ship tried to turn, but it was too late,
The ice scraped her side and sealed her fate

[Chorus]
Cold waters, cold waters, calling in the night,
A ship of dreams beneath the starry light,
Lifeboats lowered, and the band played on,
Remember the Titanic, though the ship is gone

[Bridge]
There were not enough lifeboats for all on board,
Women and children went first, as the crew implored,
The band kept playing to calm the fear,
While rockets lit the sky, calling help to come near

[Verse]
Early on April the fifteenth, she slipped from view,
The Carpathia came and rescued a few,
About seven hundred saved from the sea,
And the world changed the rules for lifeboats to be

[Chorus]
Cold waters, cold waters, a lesson learned in tears,
Lifeboats for everyone, through all the coming years,
Rockets and radios, safer ships to sail,
Remember the Titanic, and the sad, true tale
```

## Notes for new songs in this family

- Keep the 28-line shape and the medium line length above, even when the plot is rich. If a story needs more room, drop a detail, not the shape.
- Tell the real or original plot in order, one beat per couplet, and end on a lesson, a peace, or a remembrance.
- The old style prompts included timing words ("one bar intro," "vocals enter within 3 seconds"). Do not add those, because the music app may drop them. A phrase like "steady mid-tempo" is fine for this family.
